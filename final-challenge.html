🏁 The Final Challenge: Do One Real Thing

Reading about AI is like reading about swimming. This last level gets you in the water - one real task, from your actual life, done properly with a free AI tool. Beat it and you earn the final badge.

The challenge

Pick one real task from your week - something you'd have to do anyway. Good candidates: draft a tricky email, plan a study schedule, understand a confusing document, debug a piece of code, plan a trip, or explain a concept you're studying. Then work through the steps below with any free AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, or Copilot - see Models).

Tick each step off as you complete it - this page remembers your progress. Each step names the part of the guide it puts into practice.

🏁 Challenge checklist - complete all six to earn the final badge

Level-up questions (optional, but where the gold is)

Once you're done, consider: Where did the AI genuinely save you time, and where did it slow you down? What did it get wrong, and would you have caught the error without this guide? Did your second, more specific prompt produce visibly better output than your first - and what does that tell you about how you'll prompt next time?

What now? Repeat this loop with real tasks for two weeks and you'll be more capable with AI than the vast majority of users - not because you know secret tricks, but because you understand what the machine is actually doing. That was the whole point of this site. Go well.
